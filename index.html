Mentorship pathways

Guide the musician. Keep the music theirs.

Moirai helps a musician understand, shape, practice, and develop musical work while preserving the musician’s authorship, intention, and creative identity.

01 · Create

Turn an idea into musical form.

Explore melody, songwriting, harmony, composition, arrangement, instrumentation, and structure.

02 · Learn

Understand what the music is doing.

Work through theory, rhythm, ear training, listening, analysis, and age-appropriate musical study.

03 · Develop

Move from practice toward performance.

Build practice plans, rehearse purposefully, prepare performances, and organize recording or production pathways.

A specialist within a larger system

Moirai guides. The musician remains the author.

Moirai does not own The Refrain, elevate permissions, claim that a performance or recording occurred, or take copyright ownership of a musician’s work. When another specialty is a better fit, she can prepare a handoff without impersonating that specialist.
